# 🎨 Design System Update - Unique & Distinctive Look

Your app now has a **completely custom, unique design** that stands out from standard iOS apps!

## 🌈 What's Changed

### 1. **Bold New Color Palette**
- **Primary**: Indigo (`#6366F1`) - vibrant and modern
- **Accent**: Hot Pink (`#EC4899`) - eye-catching and playful
- **Gradients**: Beautiful indigo-to-pink gradients throughout
- **Shadows**: Colored shadows for depth (uses brand color instead of black)

**Before**: Standard red/gray iOS colors  
**After**: Unique indigo/pink with gradients ✨

### 2. **Gradient-Powered Buttons**
- All primary buttons now use **gradient backgrounds** (indigo → pink)
- **Colored shadows** that glow with the brand color
- **Larger** (56px instead of 48px) for better touch targets
- **More rounded** (16px radius instead of 8px)

### 3. **Typography Overhaul**
- **SF Pro Rounded** for headlines and titles (softer, friendlier)
- **Bolder weights**: Black (900) and Heavy (800) for display text
- **Display fonts** for hero sections
- **Better hierarchy** with clear size differences

### 4. **Generous Rounded Corners**
- **Cards**: 20px radius (was 12px)
- **Buttons**: 16px radius (was 8px)
- **Modals**: 24px radius (was 16px)
- Creates a softer, more modern feel

### 5. **Enhanced Shadows & Depth**
- **Colored shadows** using your brand indigo color
- **Multiple shadow styles**: small, medium, large, glow
- **Deeper shadows** for elevated cards
- Creates a unique **layered, dimensional** look

### 6. **New Card Styles**
- **Standard Card**: White with colored shadow
- **Elevated Card**: Stronger colored shadow
- **Glass Card**: Frosted glass morphism effect
- **Gradient Card**: Full gradient background with glow

### 7. **Taller Tab Bar**
- **80px height** (was 49px standard iOS)
- **Active state backgrounds** with rounded pills
- **Indicator line** at top for selected tab
- Much more distinctive than standard iOS tabs

## 🎯 Design Principles

1. **Uniqueness**: Gradient-driven design that stands out
2. **Boldness**: Heavy typography and vibrant colors
3. **Modernism**: Generous rounded corners and depth
4. **Fluidity**: Smooth animations and gradient transitions
5. **Personality**: Playful use of color and shadows
